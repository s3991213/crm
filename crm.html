Project Overview

The CRM Assistant

A smart helper built directly into the CRM. Staff ask for what they need in plain English, and it does the repetitive lookups, writing, and admin for them.

Think of it as a helpful colleague who lives inside the system. You ask a question or give an instruction, and it finds the answer, writes the reply, or updates the record for you.

How it works

STEP 1

It understands

Reads what the staff member typed and works out what they actually want.

Smart part
STEP 2

It does the work

Looks up the right records, checks the data, runs the numbers. Fixed, reliable steps.

Reliable part
STEP 3

It writes back

Turns the result into a clear answer, a short summary, or a draft email.

Smart part

The clever language part sits at the two ends. The important lookups in the middle run as fixed, dependable steps, which is what keeps the whole thing trustworthy.

What it does for staff

Answers questions instantly

Ask things like "how many students enrolled this year?" and get an answer straight away.

Summarises a student's history

A short summary of everything that has happened with a student, without the scrolling.

Drafts emails and messages

Writes follow-ups and replies for staff to quickly check and send.

Flags students who need attention

Spots students at risk of dropping out, or leads going cold, before they are lost.

Updates records on request

Just say "mark this student as enrolled" and it makes the change. No clicking through forms.

Checks its own accuracy

Regularly tested against known-correct answers, so staff can trust what it tells them.

Why it is built this way

Runs on our own computers

Student information stays in the office and is never sent out to an outside company.

Built in dependable steps

The important lookups run as fixed steps, not guesswork, so answers stay consistent and correct.
