# Credit Transfer and Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)

## Credit transfer
Students who have completed the same or an equivalent unit at another registered training organisation can get credit for it. They apply on the credit form with a certified copy of their transcript or statement of attainment. Credit transfer is free and is decided within 10 working days.

## Recognition of prior learning
RPL recognises skills and knowledge from work or life experience. The student puts together evidence (a resume, work samples, references) and may be asked to do a practical demonstration or interview with an assessor. RPL costs $250 per unit.

## When to apply
It is best to apply at enrolment, or at least 2 weeks before the unit starts. Applications after a unit has started are only accepted if the student is still attending.

## Effect on the course
Credit can shorten the course. For international students the college issues a new CoE with the new end date, and the student is told how this may affect their visa. Credit can also reduce the fees for the units that were credited.
