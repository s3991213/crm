# Student Visa Conditions and Health Cover (OSHC)

## Health cover
International students must have Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the whole time they hold a student visa. Student Services checks cover end dates and sends a reminder 30 days before a student's cover runs out. Students arrange the renewal with their insurer and send the college the new policy details.

## Working while studying
Student visa holders may work up to 48 hours per fortnight while their course is in session, and unlimited hours during scheduled course breaks. Work must not get in the way of attendance or course progress.

## Keeping details up to date
Students must tell the college about a change of address, phone number or email within 7 days. They update their details at reception or on the student portal.

## Visa extensions and new visas
A student who needs more time to finish their course must speak to Student Services before their visa ends. The college issues a new Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) if the course dates change. Students apply for the new visa themselves or through their education agent; the college can't give migration advice.

## Bridging visas
A student who has applied for a new student visa may be on a bridging visa while it is processed. They can keep studying, and must still meet the attendance and course progress rules.
