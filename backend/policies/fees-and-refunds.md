# Fees, Payment Plans and Refunds Policy

## Paying tuition fees
Tuition is paid in instalments, one per 13-week term. The first instalment and the materials fee are due with the acceptance of the letter of offer. Each later instalment is due 14 days before the term it covers starts. The non-refundable enrolment fee is $250.

## Payment plans
A student who is having trouble paying can ask the Accounts team for a payment plan before the due date. A plan can spread one instalment over up to 4 extra weeks. The plan is agreed in writing and noted on the student's record. Only one payment plan is allowed per term.

## Late and overdue payments
A reminder is sent 3 days after a due date is missed. If an instalment is more than 14 days overdue a $100 late fee applies, unless a payment plan was agreed. If it is more than 28 days overdue, the college may send a notice of intention to suspend the enrolment, and the student has 20 working days to respond or appeal.

## Refunds when a student withdraws
- Withdrawing in writing 28 days or more before the course starts: 70% of the tuition paid is refunded.
- Withdrawing less than 28 days before the start, or after the course has started: tuition already paid for the current term is not refunded. Fees paid for later terms are refunded in full.
- The enrolment fee is never refunded.

## Refunds when a visa is refused
If a student visa is refused before the course starts, the college refunds all tuition paid except $500 (or 5% of the tuition, if that is less). A copy of the visa refusal letter is needed.

## If the college can't deliver the course
If the college cancels a course or can't deliver it, students get a full refund of unspent tuition within 14 days, or can choose to be placed in another course.

## How refunds are paid
Refund claims must be made in writing on the refund form. Refunds are paid within 28 days of the claim, to the person who paid the fees, unless the student asks otherwise in writing.
