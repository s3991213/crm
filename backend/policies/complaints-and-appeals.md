# Complaints and Appeals Policy

## Raising a concern informally
Students are encouraged to talk to the staff member involved, their trainer or their counsellor first. Many problems can be sorted out quickly this way.

## Making a formal complaint or appeal
A formal complaint or appeal is made in writing, on the complaints form or by email to Student Services. Students can appeal decisions about assessment results, attendance or progress reporting, refusals of a release letter or deferral, and notices of suspension or cancellation. There is no charge.

## Timeframes
- The college acknowledges a complaint in writing within 2 working days.
- It starts looking into it within 10 working days.
- It aims to finish within 60 calendar days. If it will take longer, the student is told why and kept updated.

## During the process
The student can bring a support person to any meeting. Their enrolment stays as it is while an internal appeal is being decided. The outcome and the reasons are given in writing.

## External appeal
If the student is not satisfied with the outcome, they can take the matter to an external, independent body. International students can contact the Overseas Students Ombudsman, a free service. The college follows the external body's recommendations.

## Records
Complaints and their outcomes are kept confidential and stored for at least 5 years.
