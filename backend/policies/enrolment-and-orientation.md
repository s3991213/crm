# Enrolment and Orientation Policy

## Letter of offer
After an application is assessed, the college sends a letter of offer with the course, dates, fees and conditions. The student accepts by signing the written agreement and paying the first instalment and materials fee.

## Confirmation of Enrolment
For international students the college issues a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) once the acceptance and first payment are received, usually within 2 working days. The student needs the CoE to apply for a student visa.

## Entry requirements
International students need an English test result at the level set for the course (for example IELTS 5.5 overall for a diploma, or completing the college's English for Academic Purposes course). Vocational students need a Unique Student Identifier (USI) before they can be issued a certificate.

## Orientation
Orientation is compulsory and runs in the week before each course starts. It covers the timetable, attendance and progress rules, student support, health and safety, and visa conditions. Students who miss it must book a catch-up session with Student Services in their first week.

## Changing course
A student who wants to move to another course at the college talks to their counsellor first. A change may need a new letter of offer and, for international students, a new CoE.
