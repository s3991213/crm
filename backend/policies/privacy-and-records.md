# Privacy and Student Records Policy

## Who can see a student's information
A student's personal information, results, attendance and fee details are only given to the student. They are not given to parents or other family members, friends or employers without the student's written consent, even for students who are under 18, unless the law requires it or someone is at risk of harm.

## Education agents
An education agent can only be given information about a student who has signed the agent consent form. The consent can be withdrawn at any time.

## Checking identity
Before discussing a record over the phone or by email, staff check the student's identity with their student number and date of birth.

## Sharing with government
The college must share some information with government agencies, for example enrolment changes in PRISMS and training activity under the USI scheme. Students are told about this when they enrol.

## Keeping records
Student records are kept for at least 2 years after a student finishes or leaves, and certificates and results for 30 years. A replacement certificate costs $50.

## Correcting records
Students can ask to see their record and to correct anything that is wrong. Requests are answered within 10 working days.
