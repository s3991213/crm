# Attendance and Course Progress Policy

## Who this applies to
All students enrolled in an accredited course at the college. International students on a student visa must meet these rules as a condition of their visa, and the college must report students who don't.

## Attendance requirement
Students must attend at least 80% of the scheduled contact hours in each study period (a 13-week term). Student-visa courses are timetabled for 20 hours a week. Attendance is recorded in every class and checked every week by Student Services.

## When attendance drops
- Below 90% in a term: Student Services sends a friendly reminder email.
- Below 80%: the student receives a formal warning letter and is invited to an intervention meeting, held within 10 working days.
- At the meeting the student and their counsellor agree a support plan, usually with weekly check-ins, and the plan is noted on the student's record.

## Medical certificates and approved absences
Absences covered by a medical certificate, or approved in advance by Student Services (for example a family emergency), are still counted as absences but are taken into account when deciding what action to take. Certificates must be handed in within 5 working days of returning to class.

## Course progress
Students must be assessed as competent in at least 50% of the units they study in each term. A student who doesn't meet this is placed on an intervention strategy: extra tutoring, a study plan and re-assessment opportunities. Re-assessment is free for the first attempt and costs $75 per unit after that.

## Reporting to the government
If an international student's attendance stays below 80%, or their course progress stays unsatisfactory after the intervention, the college must notify them in writing that it intends to report them. The student has 20 working days to appeal through the complaints and appeals process. If they don't appeal, or the appeal is unsuccessful, the college reports them in PRISMS, which may affect their visa.
