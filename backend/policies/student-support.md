# Student Support and Wellbeing

## Support available
Every student has a counsellor at Student Services who helps with study plans, timetables, attendance problems and personal matters. The college also offers free, confidential counselling through an outside service (up to 6 sessions a year), study skills workshops each term, and help finding accommodation and part-time work.

## Students under 18
Students under 18 must live with a parent, a suitable relative approved by the Department of Home Affairs, or in accommodation and welfare arrangements approved by the college. Their counsellor checks in with them every 2 weeks.

## Critical incidents
A critical incident is a traumatic event, or the threat of one, such as a serious injury, assault, death, or a natural disaster. In an emergency call 000 first. Then tell the Student Services Manager straight away, who leads the college's response and records it. Staff must not talk to the media about an incident.

## Login and portal help
Problems with the student portal or learning system go to the IT helpdesk at reception or by email. Passwords are only reset after checking the student's identity.

## Referrals
Staff who are worried about a student's wellbeing should refer them to their counsellor, with the student's agreement. If someone is at immediate risk of harm, call 000.
